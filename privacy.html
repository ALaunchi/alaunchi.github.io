Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026. The Spanish version is the original; this is a faithful translation.

In short
  • You sign in through Microsoft's official page. ALaunchi never sees your password.
  • Your Microsoft/Minecraft session is stored encrypted, on your computer only.
  • Our servers keep what the social features need: your Minecraft name, profile, friends, messages and status.
  • No ads, no analytics, and we never sell data.
  • You can delete your data at any time in the app: Settings → My account → Delete my data.

1. Who is responsible

ALaunchi is a free, non-commercial Minecraft launcher developed and maintained by an individual developer, who is the data controller. For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, email alaunchi.support@gmail.com.

2. What data we process

2.1 Sign-in (Microsoft, Xbox and Mojang)

Playing requires a Minecraft: Java Edition account. Sign-in uses Microsoft's official device code flow: you type your password on Microsoft's website, never in ALaunchi. Microsoft, Xbox Live and Mojang return session tokens, your Minecraft name and UUID, your gamertag and your Microsoft account email.

  • Session tokens are encrypted with your operating system's secure storage and kept on your computer only. They are never sent to our servers.
  • Your email and gamertag are only shown on your own Settings screen. They are never sent to our servers.
  • To connect to our servers, ALaunchi proves account ownership through Mojang's session verification without sending us your token: the server only receives your Minecraft name and UUID.

2.3 Data that stays on your computer

Your instances, mods, screenshots, game logs, crash diagnosis, personal library, gallery and settings stay on your computer. Crash diagnosis analyses logs locally and sends them nowhere. If you are a modpack creator, your GitHub tokens are also stored encrypted on your computer only.

2.4 What we do not do

  • No analytics, telemetry or tracking cookies.
  • No advertising.
  • We never sell or hand over your data to anyone for commercial purposes.

3. Legal basis

  • Providing the service you request by using ALaunchi (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): sign-in, chat, friends, profile and online instances.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): automatically screening images other players see, handling reports and keeping blocks and bans, to keep the community safe.

4. Third-party services

  • Microsoft, Xbox Live and Mojang: sign-in, session verification, skins and capes. Microsoft Privacy Statement applies.
  • Google Firebase (Realtime Database, Authentication, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions): stores the data in section 2.2. The database and functions are in Europe (Belgium); uploaded files are stored in the United States.
  • Google Cloud Vision: automatically screens images other players will see for nudity or graphic violence before they are published.
  • GitHub: hosts the modpacks creators publish and ALaunchi's releases. GitHub receives your IP address when you download from it.
  • Modrinth: searching and downloading mods, shaders and resource packs. Files are identified by their hash, never by personal data. Modrinth receives your IP address when you download.
  • Discord (optional): if Discord is running, ALaunchi shows what you are playing on your Discord profile. It only talks to the Discord app on your computer and can be turned off in Settings.

Some of these services may process data outside the European Economic Area, mainly in the United States. Google, Microsoft and GitHub participate in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and use the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

5. Retention

  • Account, profile, friends and message data are kept until you delete them: you can delete messages yourself, or all your data with "Delete my data".
  • Reports and instance bans are kept for as long as moderation requires, even if you delete your data, so deleting it cannot be used to avoid a sanction.
  • Google keeps technical server logs (for example, errors) for a limited time, typically 30 days.

6. Your rights

  • Delete your data in the app: Settings → My account → Delete my data. This removes your profile, status, friends, private conversations, group chat messages, instance access and uploaded images, then signs you out.
  • Access, rectify, object to, restrict or get a copy of your data (portability) by emailing alaunchi.support@gmail.com. We reply within one month.
  • Lodge a complaint with your data protection authority; in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos.

7. Children

If you are under 14, you need permission from a parent or guardian to use ALaunchi's online features (chat, friends and profile). Parents or guardians who believe their child gave us data without permission can email us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Sessions and tokens are stored encrypted on your computer. On our servers, access rules ensure each user can only write their own data and private chats can only be read by their two participants; permissions to download private instances are stored encrypted with their access code. No system is perfect: if we detect a security issue affecting your data, we will let you know.

9. Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above, and announce important changes in the app.
